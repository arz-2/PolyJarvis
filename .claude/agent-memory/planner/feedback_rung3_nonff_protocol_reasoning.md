On PMMA1's rung-3 re-plan (density -5.40% after 2 cooling-rate recovery sub-attempts, quench-rate saturation diagnosed by recovery-agent), the orchestrator explicitly invited reasoning about whether rung 3 should actually be an FF swap or a non-FF protocol/acceptance decision, rather than mechanically executing recover.md's "full re-plan with a different force field" text.

**Why:** Two pieces of already-encoded evidence made an FF swap indefensible here: (1) Tang2022 (OPLS-AA, the only EMC-routed PACR alternative) shows *worse or equal* cooling-rate coupling than PCFF (+98 K Tg overshoot even at 1 K/ns) — primary-source evidence that the defect is FF-generic quench-rate physics, not a PCFF parameterization gap; (2) `decision_policy.json`'s own `density_value_binding` rationale cites this exact PMMA failure mode by name and states NkepsuMbitou2025 reached the experimental density target via ~100 ns of melt annealing — but a deeper read of `eq_annealing_cycles_is_planning_only` revealed the plan's own "10-cycle" fix hypothesis was **never actually wired into the runtime tool** (`generate_equilibration_workflow` has no N-cycle argument), so that hypothesis was never really tested. Both facts only surface by reading `decision_policy.json` rationale prose closely, not by pattern-matching the recover.md ladder step name.

**How to apply:** When a STRUCTURAL_FAIL ladder rung names a category of fix ("different force field", "re-melt", etc.), verify the fix actually addresses the *diagnosed* defect (per recovery-agent's specific finding — e.g. "liquid-leg cooling kinetics" vs "melt equilibration") before authoring it into the plan. Check whether class-default "hypothesis" fields (like `eq_annealing_cycles`) are actually enacted by the runtime tool before crediting or blaming them — `decision_policy.json:policies.equilibration.eq_annealing_cycles_is_planning_only` is the canonical statement that this field is planning-only. A rung-3 re-plan may legitimately conclude "accept a rate-limited result, documented as an explicit exception" rather than swap FF — but that exception must be recorded as a *stated deviation* from the binding gate (with the correct diagnosis code, e.g. UNDER_ANNEALED_COOLING, not silently reclassified to a more permissive one like MELT_STAGE_DEFICIT to borrow its acceptance clause).
