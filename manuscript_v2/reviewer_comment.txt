Comments:
The revised manuscript is substantially improved. The expanded benchmark, experiment-based scorecard, revised title and scope, ensemble-based presentation, additional structural diagnostics, and more restrained conclusions address many of the previous comments. Nevertheless, several issues central to the manuscript’s claims of statistical reliability and autonomous workflow execution remain unresolved.

Replicate design and Tg robustness. The four runs for a given polymer are not consistently independent repeats under a fixed protocol. Cooling rate, chain length, atom count, chain count, annealing cycles, and/or the equation-of-state pressure ladder vary in several systems. Consequently, the reported mean and standard deviation conflate stochastic sampling uncertainty with protocol and finite-size effects. The authors should either perform fixed-protocol simulations with independent seeds or explicitly relabel the present runs as protocol variants and analyze the effects separately. Controlled Tg sensitivity tests with respect to cooling rate, temperature grid, equilibration duration, and fitting procedure remain necessary for representative systems.
Application of the convergence gate. Several trajectories appear to exceed the stated hard thresholds for energy drift, radius-of-gyration variability, or density homogeneity, yet they remain included in the property ensembles. A per-run table should report the pass/fail status of every gate, the action taken after failure, and the final disposition of the trajectory. Runs that fail a declared hard criterion should be rerun or excluded according to a predeclared rule. Exceptions should be justified explicitly rather than omitted selectively from diagnostic averages. The corrected PE result also does not demonstrate that the original over-densification would have been autonomously detected and repaired.
Agent contribution and recovery benchmark. The present benchmark primarily demonstrates orchestration, catalog-based troubleshooting, and diagnosis. The strongest “agent-only” cases should be shown to resume and complete the intended end-to-end workflow rather than stopping after identifying a rerouting or rebuilding strategy. Recovery should be defined as successful completion of the intended simulation and property calculation, tested over repeated trials and preferably on unseen failure cases. An end-to-end deterministic workflow baseline is still needed to quantify the incremental contribution of the LLM.
Force-field and charge-model validation. The requested sensitivity analysis has not been performed. Given the systematic density bias of the PCFF systems and the PEG bulk-modulus error, controlled calculations using an alternative force field or charge treatment should be provided for at least one or two representative systems. The force-field routing description must also be made internally consistent. Until these tests are available, attribution of the observed errors specifically to PCFF should be presented as a hypothesis rather than a demonstrated mechanism.
Bulk-modulus robustness. The added autocorrelation, effective-sample-size, and fit statistics are useful. However, actual sensitivity to pressure-ladder span, barostat settings, production length, and system size has not been demonstrated. At least one representative sensitivity calculation should be provided, or the assertion that the equation-of-state result is insensitive to barostat choice should be substantially qualified.
Reproducibility. The exact code, prompts, tool schemas, inputs, seeds, structures, processed data, and analysis scripts corresponding to the reviewed manuscript should be deposited as an immutable tagged and archived release with a permanent identifier. The manuscript and repository should also be reconciled regarding the availability of notebooks, scripts, trajectories, and raw data, and the precise commit used to generate the reported results should be stated.

These remaining issues affect the interpretation of the reported uncertainties and the central claim of autonomous scientific workflow execution. They should be resolved before the manuscript is considered for publication.
